# Privacy Policy

**Ogygia** ("the App") is developed by Ryuichi. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

## Data Collection

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

- **GitHub Personal Access Token** — stored securely in the iOS Keychain
- **Repository configuration** (owner, repo name, branch, image repo, CDN prefix) — stored in UserDefaults
- **Publishing history** — stored as a local JSON file
- **Essay cache** — stored temporarily in the app's cache directory

All data remains on your device and is never sent to any server other than GitHub's API, which you configure yourself.

## Third-Party Services

The App communicates directly with the **GitHub API** (api.github.com) using credentials you provide. The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all App data at any time by using the "Reset All Settings" option within the App, or by deleting the App from your device.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted here.
